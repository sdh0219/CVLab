Dr. Jing Yuan is an Associate Professor at the College of Computer Science and Engineering, University of Emergency Management (UEM), where she leads the lab. Her research sits at the intersection of artificial intelligence and geophysics, covering intelligent processing of electromagnetic satellite data, video-based seismology, and industrial vision inspection.

## Education

- Ph.D., Tsinghua University — Information and Communication Engineering (computer vision), 2011.09–2018.12
- M.Eng., Central China Normal University — Circuits and Systems (intelligent signal processing)
- B.Eng., Hebei University of Science and Technology — Communication Engineering

## Career

- Teaching Assistant / Lecturer, Institute of Disaster Prevention
- Associate Professor, University of Emergency Management (formerly Institute of Disaster Prevention)

## Professional Service

- Member, China Computer Federation (CCF)
- Committee member, Commission on Artificial Intelligence, Chinese Geophysical Society

## Honors

- Outstanding Party Member, China Earthquake Administration affiliates, 2021
- Excellent Teacher, Institute of Disaster Prevention, 2021

## Prospective Students

She supervises graduate students in Geophysical Exploration and Information Technology. Students interested in computer vision, deep learning, and their geophysical applications are welcome to join the lab.
