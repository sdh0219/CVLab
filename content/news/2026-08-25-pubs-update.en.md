Several recent research outputs from the lab have been published:

- Evidence-guided Bayesian network structure learning, in *Expert Systems with Applications* (SCI Q1 Top journal);
- Automatic extraction of whistler-wave propagation parameters, proton whistler crossing-frequency identification, and strong-motion information extraction from surveillance video, in *Chinese Journal of Geophysics*;
- Short-term strong-earthquake hazard probability prediction via Bayesian network structure learning, in *Earth Science*;
- A rapid recognition model for lightning-generated whistlers with parallel bidirectional SRU network, in *Remote Sensing*.
