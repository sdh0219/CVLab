Dr. Jing Yuan is an Associate Professor at the College of Computer and Information Security, University of Emergency Management (UEM), where she leads the lab. Her research sits at the intersection of artificial intelligence and geophysics, covering intelligent processing of electromagnetic satellite data, video-based seismology and intelligent earthquake monitoring, and industrial vision inspection.

## Education

- Ph.D., Department of Electronic Engineering, Tsinghua University, 2011.09–2019.01
- M.Eng., School of Physics and Technology, Central China Normal University, 2004.09–2007.06
- B.Eng., School of Information Science and Technology, Hebei University of Science and Technology, 1999.09–2003.06
