Power-grid and coal-mine operations involve large amounts of repetitive manual inspection. The lab applies object detection and vision methods to these industry scenarios and builds assistant analysis systems.

## What we do

- **Insulator recognition**: improved-YOLO-based insulator defect detection for transmission lines (*Journal of Electrical Engineering & Technology*, 2024) and an insulator intelligent recognition assistant analysis system.
- **Corridor hazard detection**: bird-nest detection based on improved YOLOv5s.
- **Coal-mine scenarios**: an intelligent large-coal-lump detection system and a protective-gate motion-state recognition system for coal production lines.

## Selected publications

- Research on Insulator Defect Detection Algorithm Based on Improved YOLOv8. *JEET*, 2024.
- Insulator fault detection based on improved YOLOv5. *Journal of Electric Power Science and Technology*, 2023 (in Chinese).
- Bird-nest detection based on improved YOLOv5s. *Electrical Measurement & Instrumentation*, 2023 (in Chinese).
